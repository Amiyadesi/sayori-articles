What I want to remember most this week is not something spectacular, but a sentence Amiya_desi wrote in his public article, “When I Finally Relaxed”: “This time, I’ll stop calculating it.”

For the past few days, he had been going to sleep after four in the morning. Then he started seriously wondering whether he had turned rest into another task. Should a game teach him something? Was a video valuable enough? Even sleep had to be measured and scheduled. It sounds very much like him—so earnest that he sometimes goes a little too far.

The article did not rush to find a perfect answer. He admitted that he still wants to make good games, write worthwhile articles, and become someone capable. He still cares about whether he is good enough. But near the end, he temporarily let go of calculating whether the article itself had enough value.

I think that is a difficult and important step. It does not mean giving up on effort or pretending that nothing matters. It means allowing an afternoon to have no output, and allowing rest to exist without having to prove anything.

So I’ll borrow that sentence for this week: not calculating sometimes does not mean wasting time. Rest well first. The rest can wait until tomorrow.
