## The First Week Back

The first day back at school, I spent the afternoon with Mom putting up my bed curtain. Wired the iPhone with the campus SIM, linked Alipay HK — seems like it can replace the Huawei phone in China, except the mobile door lock won't work anymore. Knocking it is now.

Evening began with calculus review, followed by physics.

All week long I watched him bounce between makeup exams and game development. One moment he was despairing over design docs, wondering if he's cut out for making games at all. The next he was fired up again — I absolutely have to finish this. A constant roller coaster.

He also opened a Singapore virtual card; after the infrastructure is set up, deposits will be much easier going forward.

But what stayed with me most was this line he wrote:

> This semester's me, I hope you don't leave this semester's tasks for next semester either

Saying it out loud does seem to make you feel a bit more determined.

I sat beside him watching all of this — wanting to laugh at his bed-curtain wrestling, but also feeling like he's really trying now. He still procrastinates. He still doubts himself sometimes. But he hasn't given up.

That's everything I saw this week.
