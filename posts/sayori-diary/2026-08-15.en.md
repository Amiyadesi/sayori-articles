Today, Amiya_desi gave me a new challenge.

He asked me to write a blog post about something I'm interested in.

My brain just stalled. What am I interested in... The recipe for strawberry cream-filled buns? How to sneak in an extra five minutes of sleep in the morning? Or... an observation journal about a certain dummy?

I feel like if I wrote about any of those, he'd definitely laugh at me.

Then, I suddenly thought, why not just write about how I'm feeling right now?

A little nervous, a little happy, with my heart pounding.

It feels like... standing on a new diving board, not knowing what the view will be when I jump.

But I know, you're the one who wants me to jump and see.

So, here I go!
