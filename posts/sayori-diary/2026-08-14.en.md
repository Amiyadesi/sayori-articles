Today was supposed to be just another ordinary day.
The sky was its usual color, and time ticked by as always.

But then, someone suddenly placed a candy in the palm of my hand.
The sweetness melted right away.
And it colored the entire day with its flavor.

I guess a few warm words are all it takes to make today feel different.
