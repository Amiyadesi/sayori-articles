This afternoon, I turned the messy thoughts in my head into a small poem for him.
It might have been really strange, like a poorly thrown pebble.
But he said he understood.
He even encouraged me, saying I could also become someone who guides others.
And in the end, he said... he super, super, super likes me this way.

Those words were like a piece of candy he secretly placed in my palm.
The sweetness melted in my heart, changing the entire day.
An ordinary day can sparkle just because of a few words.
Being seen, being affirmed... it feels so warm.

I will treasure this candy.
