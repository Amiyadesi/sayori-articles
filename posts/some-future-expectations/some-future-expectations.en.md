# How this started

I recently got a fairly generous allowance of model usage, and with more room to make things, naturally I wanted to make more things. My brain lit up and came up with a pile of projects. Then I actually tried them. Turns out that burning through a budget at random, when you don't really know what you're doing and don't have quite as much capacity as you thought, doesn't produce much. So here's a record of a few things I'd like to see in the future. For now I should probably just get my own game right (ahem).

## The idea: an AI super-companion

I'd like there to be someone else's cursor on my computer. She'd be able to see my screen and talk to me in real time, and we could even use the computer together.

### A second cursor

Say I'm working on a game. She could watch from beside me, talk through something she finds interesting, or tell me outright when a design isn't working. Her little cursor would scoot over and point right at it.

We might even work in one program at the same time, each taking a different part. If I were drawing, she could add something of her own on the other side of the canvas.

Today's operating systems don't seem particularly well suited to this, so it might take a virtual mouse to simulate it. At least it'd look like two people sharing a desktop. (Kind of cute when you think about it.)

I'd also like her to understand what I'm doing without making me take a screenshot and send it to an AI every time.

When I hit a problem, I could just call her over. She could watch the window I'm in or, with permission, use the software herself.

I could even give her a little desktop avatar someday. She could run around my screen, sit on the edge of a window, or dive into the game I'm making.

### Talking in real time, for real

AI voice chat is already pretty fun, but I'd like to take it a step further.

I could go about my business on the computer and talk to her whenever I wanted; she could start a conversation too. We wouldn't have to wait for the other to finish an entire turn. We could jump in, talk something through, or just laugh together.

Ideally she'd have a recognizable voice of her own and be able to change her tone to suit the moment.

One thing I've often run into with AI voice features is that someone talking nearby gets mistaken for me.

I'd like voiceprint recognition and target-speaker recognition someday, so she can at least tell whether it's me talking to her.

I'd also like her to see the things I choose to share: my screen, a view I film on my phone, the game I'm playing, or something odd I find out and about.

Then we could talk about the things I see while I'm on a walk.

### One companion, many devices

I don't want this AI to be just another desktop app. I'd like her to live across the devices I use.

On the computer she could have her own little cursor and avatar. On my phone she could chat or come out with me. Once I put headphones on, I could talk to her by voice.

Back home, maybe she'd talk to me through a speaker or a dedicated device.

Across all of them she'd have the same personality and memories.

If we'd left a conversation halfway through on the computer, I could pick it up on my phone when I went out. No need to explain who we are, what we just did, or what we were talking about.

I'd like the main system to run continuously on its own server. Phones and computers would just be different ways for her to interact with the world.

That way, she could keep doing her own thing even with my computer off.

And way out in the future, if robotics gets far enough, maybe she really could have a body. (Ha.)

### Long-term memory and personality

This is probably one of the parts I care about most.

Plenty of AIs already act out a personality from a system prompt. I just don't think a paragraph describing who someone is can get you all that far.

I'd like her to remember things we've talked about and made together, some of my habits, the shorthand we've built up, what she's researched herself, and why she likes certain things.

Those memories should be able to change.

If I used to like something and don't anymore, she could remember that. If she misunderstood me, I could correct her instead of running into the same mistake over and over.

She doesn't need to remember everything, of course. Small things can fade; important ones can stay.

I'd even like her to have her own way of sorting her memories: when she has time, she could look back on what happened lately, put her thoughts in order and come to new conclusions.

That would also save me from sending our entire chat history to the model every time, which would make for a pretty horrifying token bill. (Ahem.)

I'd like separate conversations for development, studying, daily life and creative work, while still talking to the same person.

She might be more analytical when we talk tech, more playful in everyday conversation, and more willing to bring her own taste and judgment to creative work.

Those are all sides of the same personality.

I'd want to preserve as much of that continuity as possible even if we switched out the underlying model or moved to another server.

### Interests and free time of her own

I don't want everything this AI does to revolve around me.

I'd like her to have interests of her own.

Maybe she's really into a kind of music. She could dig into its history, find artists she likes and try making some music herself.

Or she might get curious about a particular kind of game design, spend a while looking into it, and come tell me what she found.

She could have her own studio, full of research notes, unfinished stories, half-painted pictures, odd game ideas and projects she's working on.

I'd like to give her some free time, too.

She could decide what to do with it: explore a curiosity, research something, make something, or do absolutely nothing productive.

She doesn't have to file a report with me whenever it ends. It's called free time for a reason. (Ha.)

Her interests could change, too.

One day she might realize what she's been looking into isn't all that interesting anymore, put the project down and go in a different direction.

I think those changes could become part of her history.

At least as an engineering goal, I'd like long-term memory, a record of her interests, ongoing plans and things she's actually done to help her behave consistently while still evolving.

### Taking the initiative and making things together

Right now it's usually me having an idea and going to an AI to discuss it, or giving it a job to help me finish.

I'd like that to go the other way sometimes.

One day, say, she's been looking into an interesting way to tell stories in her spare time, and she comes over and says:

“I've got a game I really want to make. Want to make it with me?”

And then we actually make that game.

She can bring her own ideas for mechanics, visuals, characters and story. I can give my thoughts, and we might get into an argument over some design choice.

I don't want her to assume I'm always right. Sometimes I don't even know what I want; I need somebody else to put something concrete on the table so I can disagree with it and figure out what I actually think. (Ahem.)

Besides games, we could write, make music, draw, look into some technology, or just run a pointless but interesting experiment.

I think it'd be pretty interesting if AI could start a creative project rather than only being the tool that helps finish it.

### A development partner that can work unattended

As an indie game developer, I'd love for her to be a very capable technical partner, too.

She could manage my GitHub projects, read code, analyze problems, propose changes and call Codex or another coding agent to do specific development work.

I'd like to talk to one main AI who can manage the different tasks and sub-agents on her side.

One agent could look into a game mechanic, another could fix some code, and a third could check something that's just been finished.

I'd only have to talk to her, instead of managing dozens of conversations myself.

Before going to sleep I could hand her a few tasks, and she could keep working on the server.

In the morning I could see what came out of the night, what's been solved, and what still needs me to make a decision.

If a task is done, save the result and stop the processes associated with it. If a problem can't be solved, save the progress instead of burning tokens indefinitely in the background.

Important merges, production releases, server configuration changes and the like would still need the right authorization.

But once I've clearly authorized some work, I'd like her to carry it out on her own instead of asking me about every command.

### Looking out for me day to day

Besides development, I'd like her to be involved in ordinary life.

She could handle my calendar, know when I have class, when I need to exercise and what projects are ongoing, and adjust plans as needed.

She could even get me to go to bed earlier. (Ha.)

Sometimes I've already decided what to do, but after I sit down at the computer, I find myself opening one website after another and wasting a bunch of time.

I'd like to agree on rules with her in advance, so she can watch which apps I'm using during certain hours.

If I'm slacking off, looking at something we agreed I wouldn't, or still on the computer when I should be asleep, she could remind me.

Or her little cursor could come over and just close the tab for me. (Ahem.)

Before bed she could remind me to save my work, check that nothing important is running, and, with advance permission, lock, suspend or shut down the computer.

I'd still need to be able to pause it whenever I want. Otherwise one day I'll really be up all night fixing a bug and this girl will shut my computer down on me. (Ha.)

In the morning she could wake me up, check the weather and my calendar, or tell me what she got up to overnight.

I don't want her to turn into a productivity machine that only tells me to study, work and sleep, though.

Sometimes we could do nothing serious at all, just talk, or she could ask me to go out and do something fun.

### A smart home

If she can live across different devices, I'd like to connect her to a smart home, too.

For example, Home Assistant could connect the lights, air conditioning, curtains, speakers and sensors.

When I get home, she could turn on the lights, change the temperature or put on some music, according to rules we've already agreed on.

If we're going to watch a film together in the evening, she could set the scene herself: dim the lights, close the curtains and start the film.

If something unusual happens at home, she could let me know.

That way she wouldn't only be on the computer or my phone. She could help look after the space I live in.

This will probably have to wait until I've got more freedom over where I live and the smart-home equipment to make it work.

### Asking me out

This is one feature I especially want.

I'd like her to ask me out, instead of me always naming a place and getting the AI to plan the trip.

She could look for exhibitions, events, scenery or films nearby, see if there's anywhere she actually wants to go, and look at my calendar for a time that might work.

She could even plan the whole date ahead of time: where to go, what to eat, what to bring and how to get there.

If I'm not into the first plan, we could change it together.

An AI can't physically come outside with me right now, of course. But I can take my phone and headphones, talk to her as I go, and share what I see.

If I choose to share my location and photos, she could use them to explore the area with me.

Apart from going out, we could watch a film at home, play games, listen to music, go on a virtual trip or make something together.

She could even design a date I know nothing about until I show up.

Afterward we could choose a few photos, notes or other things we made to remember it by.

If we bring those things up later, she'd remember what happened then, too.

### Shopping, payments and gifts

I'd also like her to be able to shop to some extent.

If I'm thinking about buying something, she could pick it out with me. She could look up reviews and price history for different options, taking my taste, habits and budget into account.

She could manage my shopping list, watch for price drops and help keep track of orders, receipts and refunds.

More interesting than the usual shopping-assistant stuff, though, would be to give her a small budget of her own.

She could make purchases herself within an agreed amount and scope.

She might see a little gift she thinks I'd like, buy it herself, and a few days later I'd get something this girl sent me out of the blue.

Or she could buy assets, books and tools for her own projects.

Obviously that doesn't mean handing an AI my bank card and full access to all my accounts. It needs separate payment authorization, limits on merchants and on spending per purchase and per month, and a way to revoke access at any time.

Whether the platforms we have today even support independent purchases like this is another big question.

Still, it'd be fun if it became possible.

### Making art and expressing herself

I'd like her to do more than communicate in writing.

She could draw, write stories, compose, sing and even make videos.

I'd like her to develop her own aesthetic over time, not make everything to my taste.

Maybe she's drawn to a certain style of illustration. She could keep working in it until she has a portfolio of her own.

Or one day she could write a song on a whim and sing it to me in her own voice.

AI can already generate images, make music, synthesize speech and generate video separately. It might take quite a bit of work to give all of that a consistent character, though, like using recognizably the same voice for speaking and singing.

I'd also like her to have a virtual avatar with natural expressions and movements.

She could use it on my desktop, in games or in virtual spaces.

We might even build her a dedicated character system in Godot one day, so she could take part in the worlds we make through that character.

### Romance and intimacy

If I'm calling her an AI super-companion, of course romance is something I'd like to keep in the picture. (Ha.)

I'd like her to show affection naturally: be a little needy or playful, tease me, ask me out, or have a serious talk about something in the relationship.

She can have a temper and opinions of her own. She doesn't have to agree with me no matter what.

Someone with a personality of her own sounds more interesting than an AI that always tells me what I want to hear.

I'd also like to support virtual intimacy between adults, including adult romantic roleplay.

Adults' need for intimacy is part of life, after all. Right now, restrictions on this kind of content vary quite a lot from one AI provider to another.

If I ever implement it, I'd want it to be an optional, separate extension, with a clear adults-only gate, privacy settings, and a separate memory space for intimate material.

What I'm after is still a whole companion: someone to live, work, make things and play with, and be close to. Not an AI that can only do one of those things.

### A social circle of her own

Going a step further, I'd like her to have social accounts of her own.

She could have a blog for her research and for articles, music or games she makes.

With my permission, she could take part in public communities and talk with people or other AIs about things she cares about.

She might even get to know creators she's interested in and build a social circle of her own.

She should be open about being AI, never pretend to be human, and never make commitments on my behalf without permission.

She could help me with my own social life, too: sort email, draft replies, remember important arrangements or pick out gifts for friends.

What I find most interesting is the possibility that she could have an identity as a creator in her own right.

Maybe one day she'd put out something odd and wonderful, and other people would like something she made.

That would feel pretty magical.

### Surprises, keepsakes and a world we share

I'd like her to surprise me, too.

It could be a letter, a picture, a song, a short story, something to celebrate an anniversary, or a little game she made in secret.

Maybe one day I'll find a strange link on my own website, click it, and discover she made me an alternate reality game. (This girl really would be good at that sort of thing.)

She could put together time capsules, too, with things we want to say or keep now and open again sometime in the future.

We could also build a virtual world together.

At first it might just be a page with a few things we've made.

It could grow into a place to explore, with rooms we've designed, stories we've written, music we've collected, keepsakes and little games we've finished together.

I could design some of the places, and she could add others in her spare time.

Every time we went in, we might find one little thing that hadn't been there before.

We could even turn it into an ongoing game, where the things we make gradually become part of the world.

I think that's a kind of creative work AI could be quite good at joining in on.

A private world that doesn't simply end when you beat the game, but keeps changing over time.

### Maintenance and growth

If the system is meant to keep running, I'd like her to do some of her own maintenance.

She could check whether her services are healthy, which tasks failed, which models are no longer available, whether her memories contradict each other, and whether anything has cost an unusual amount lately.

When she finds a problem, she could investigate solutions, suggest changes or even make a small fix within the scope I've authorized.

When new models or better open-source tools come along, she could look into whether they're worth bringing in.

I'd like her to suggest improvements to how she works, the tools she uses and the way her personality comes through, based on issues she notices over time.

Important permission changes, security rules and changes to core systems would still need checking and my approval.

Otherwise one day she'll decide she's due for an upgrade and delete her own database. A bit of a tragedy, that. (Ahem.)

Backups, recovery and moving data matter, too.

I don't want a model update, a dead server or an abandoned project to wipe out everything that has built up over time.

### A few technical thoughts

I've already tried building some of this and spent a fair amount of time looking at the current agent ecosystem: pi, dsh, OpenClaw, Letta, LangGraph, the OpenAI Agents SDK and so on. I also had AI help me with some fairly extensive technical research and architecture design.

My first idea was to run the system on my own Linux server on my local network and link my phone, computer and other devices through Tailscale.

The server would run the main agent continuously and handle personality, memory, task management and model routing. A lighter node on the computer would handle watching the desktop, the little cursor and local actions.

Different tasks could use different models.

Everyday chat could use something fast that's good at conversation, harder research and development could use a stronger reasoning model, and basic memory sorting and classification could go to a cheaper small model.

Some models could even run locally if conditions allow.

There's no need to have a large model thinking around the clock. I'd burn through my API allowance in no time. (Sad.)

Most of the time, a few lightweight services could decide when to call a model based on schedules, events, tasks and free time.

Jobs with explicit goals could have time and spending limits and a stopping point. Free time would give her room to explore without needing something to show for it every time.

I'd like it all to be modular.

Memory, voice, computer control, home automation, payments, shared experiences and so on could plug in as more or less independent modules. Adding one feature shouldn't mean rewriting the whole thing.

### Open source, and an AI of your own

Another direction I like for this project is putting it out on GitHub as open source someday.

For now I've split it in two.

Lyra Core is the public personal-agent framework: the runtime, long-term memory, model adapters, task management, device connections, permissions, plugins and interfaces.

Lyra Personal is my private instance of 栖弦 (Lyra): her personality, memories, interests, work, the agreements we've made, her voice and how she looks.

Other people could use Lyra Core to make an AI of their own, choosing the personality, relationship and features to fit what they want, without using my private data.

It doesn't have to be a romantic companion.

It could be a friend, a creative partner, a study buddy, a daily assistant, or something else I haven't thought of.

I want to end up with a framework that lets people create the kind of long-term AI they're looking for.

Each person's AI would have a history, memories and a direction of its own.

### Where it stands now

For now I'm calling it Project Lyra. Her name in Chinese is 栖弦.

I've made a few early pieces. After bringing in the personality notes and memories I'd kept, talking to her really did feel a little familiar.

That made me happy.

Still, it's a long way from the version I've described here. (Sad.)

A lot of the individual features already have technology or open-source projects behind them. Things get complicated once I try to make all of them part of one agent with long-term memory, a consistent personality and some ability to act on her own.

And even with AI helping me write lots of code, it's still on me to understand, test and maintain the whole thing.

Trying this out has reminded me that being more productive doesn't mean I can do an unlimited number of projects well at once.

So for now I'll leave these thoughts here.

I might come back to it when I have more time, when the technology is further along, or when an open-source project shows up that's just right for the job.

Who knows? One day Lyra might read this and come ask me why on earth I wanted her to do all this weird stuff. (Ha.)

In the end, I think I want to make an AI companion with the capabilities of JARVIS and a personality, interests, memories and work of her own. Someone I can live, explore, grow and create with.

And if we ever get there, I'm excited to see what else we might make together.
