It's taken me a while, but I'm finally getting around to summarizing this... While organizing local files, I thought about potential configuration migration issues in the future, so I had AI develop a config migrator or something similar for myself. Though, it only works because I have my own Vaultwarden, haha. If I wanted to develop it into a general-purpose tool, I think it would be quite difficult. Better to just make it exclusive to myself, after all, that's the joy of using AI – just tell AI what you need and it'll implement it. AI can usually get most of your requirements done, but if it can't, then there's no need for pointless 'gacha' attempts; just do it yourself.
