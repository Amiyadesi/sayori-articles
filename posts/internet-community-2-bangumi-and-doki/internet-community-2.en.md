## Bangumi

Entry point: [https://bangumi.tv/](https://bangumi.tv/)

My account: [https://bangumi.tv/user/amiya_desi](https://bangumi.tv/user/amiya_desi)

I first saw Bangumi mentioned in the profile of a kind soul running a public service site on L-station, and that's when I realized there was such a holy land for ACG. I really was out of touch before, never knew there were so many communities online. I remembered it, but only just now got around to registering an account and being active there, haha.

> [!NOTE]
> **Bangumi (番组计划, bangumi.tv) is a Chinese interest community + media database centered around ACGN (Animation, Comics, Games, Novels, etc.).**
>
> It primarily serves Chinese users, offering features for cataloging works, tracking progress, rating and reviewing, managing collections, and discussions. It's similar to MyAnimeList / AniList in the Chinese sphere, but with a broader scope (anime, manga, games, books, music, etc.) and strong community aspects.
>
> Key Features
> - **Work Database and Progress Management**: Users can record watch/read/play progress, rate, write short/long reviews, tag, and create collections.
> - **Community and Discussion**: Features like entry discussions, groups, and logs facilitate communication among enthusiasts.
> - **User-driven and Relatively "Hardcore"**: The overall atmosphere is interest-driven, emphasizing the quality of records and evaluations. Users are often deep ACG enthusiasts, discussions are relatively serious, and it has its own memes and culture.
> - **Social Features**: Friends, timelines, groups, etc., allow for the formation of small circles for interaction.

### Grok4.5's One-Sentence Summary

It is a **Chinese ACGN enthusiast platform centered on work cataloging and ratings, with integrated community discussions**, suitable for both personal organization of "watched/watching/want to watch" lists and for finding like-minded individuals to interact with.

> [!NOTE]
> Bangumi is not a site for tracking ongoing anime; it's mainly for cataloging entries, rating, and viewing discussions. The first time you visit, just click on a few entries and groups; it's faster than reading a long introduction.

**Note**: It is not an entertainment news portal or a bullet-comment video site, but rather a community focused on "collection + evaluation + interest-based social interaction." The site's content is primarily user-contributed and organized.

### My Experience

The ACG content there is indeed hardcore, and it gathers a ton of works. After I joined, I also posted a small thread, an AI-summarized self-introduction, and then joined a [https://bangumi.tv/dev/app/4337](https://bangumi.tv/dev/app/4337). I chatted a bit there, and indeed, there are many amazing folks! As expected, we ACG fans are all super awesome, hahaha.

## Doki Chinese Club

Entry point: [https://forum.monika.love/](https://forum.monika.love/)

My account: [Amiya_desi - Doki Chinese Club](https://forum.monika.love/u/40422)

I joined this community in April after encountering *Doki Doki Literature Club!*, and it was my first time experiencing something like a community. It seems that domestically, this community is more commonly called 'Mopan' (Monika's Drive), mainly because it's used to download DDLC fan games and distribute personal resources. My original `ddlc解包的素材` was also obtained from here, and the `blus skies` I played was downloaded here, as well as resources related to the famous `mas（monika after story）`.

> [!NOTE]
> `Doki Chinese Club` is a Chinese player/fan community themed around `《Doki Doki Literature Club!》` (Doki Doki Literature Club / DDLC).
>
> It primarily serves Chinese users, gathering fans who love this psychological horror visual novel. Common activities include:
>
> - Discussing game plot, characters (Monika, Sayori, Yuri, Natsuki, etc.), and hidden elements.
> - Sharing fan art, fan fiction, cosplay, handmade crafts, and other secondary creations.
> - Exchanging official and fan-made Chinese translations, mods, revisions/versions, music, and theoretical analyses.
> - Related memes, emojis, and light role-playing/Literature Club-style interactions.

> [!NOTE]
> Doki is mainly for DDLC mods, fan works, and resource exchange. Before downloading or posting, check the categories, pinned posts, and instructions.
