## GitHub List Repositories

## Free Domains and Temporary Entry Points

- [DNSHE](https://my.dnshe.com/): Free subdomain, good for practicing DNS first.
- [eu.org](https://nic.eu.org/): Classic free domain, but approval is often slow.
- [us.kg](https://nic.us.kg/): Also quite common, check current rules before registering.
- [pp.ua](https://nic.ua/): One of the established free domain entry points.
- [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/): Will give you a `github.io`.
- [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/): Will give you a `pages.dev`.

If you're just starting a website, these should keep you busy for a while.

## Official Domain Registration

- [Namesilo](https://www.namesilo.com/)
- [Porkbun](https://porkbun.com/)
- [Cloudflare Registrar](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/registrar/)

I personally prioritize checking the first-year price and renewal price, otherwise it's easy to get lured in by a low first-year cost.

## Static Hosting

- [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/): I'm currently using it myself.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/): Many front-end projects are hosted here.
- [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/)
- [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/)
- [Render](https://render.com/)

For a purely static blog, Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages are generally sufficient.
