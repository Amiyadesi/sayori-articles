Oh my god, this is terrifying. I feel like the target audience for these "hand-drawn animations" (手书) is a bunch of people I honestly don't quite get. They seem to be mostly girls, the fan-fic type, with lots of rules, and they love writing all sorts of bizarrely long comments. It feels a bit too enthusiastic... a bit scary, actually. And right now, I feel like what I'm making is more like a music PV or a forbidden-style MAD. If I were to make a character "hand-drawn animation," I'd have to find a ton of materials myself, because I'm not much of an artist, so I'd just piece together official assets. But everyone else's "hand-drawn animations" seem to be entirely self-drawn. I'm so scared people will nitpick it. It's terrifying, I'm panicking, I want to give up, but giving up without even trying? I'm such a failure, damn it. Plus, the character combination I came up with was a sudden inspiration, and I've found nothing similar on the entire internet. I'm afraid it won't be understood, or even worse, get flamed. Sigh...

After asking an AI, I realized that what I'm making is indeed better described as a MAD or a PV, and shouldn't be called a "hand-drawn animation." Before, I didn't know and just assumed everything was a "hand-drawn animation." Well, I guess I learned something new, huh? haha.
