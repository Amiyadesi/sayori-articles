Lumi, Omilia, Tutorial Captain, Paper Girl, Imposter Sock (I think that's what they were called), Mr. Sock, the male protagonist and his lover, plus the classic cheat code/golden finger – a three-hour playthrough created so many distinct characters, it's incredible.

The game can roughly be divided into four stages: files 1, 2, 3, and finally hacking the system. The classic "introduction, development, turn, and conclusion" never goes out of style.

Gameplay is basically simple decryption when finding files, then decrypting the files is a medium-level decryption, and after entering memories, it's story, platforming, and combat. It's well-layered.
