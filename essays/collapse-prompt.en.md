I'm so annoyed right now. The first act, which was running pretty well before, has now been ruined by AI. And looking at that first act now, it just gets worse and worse. Plus, there are a ton of bugs, and the content length is totally inadequate. I'm a bit overwhelmed. I doubt if I can even do this well. In the end, I'm probably not even as good as those ordinary people who just play games all day. I just seem like someone independent, clear-headed, and with a plan, but in reality, I'm no different from those who lack ambition, just purely moving myself. What's the point of making indie games? Learning this and that. Will I find a job after learning all this? Will I make big money? ....But I still want to make games. I even want to learn skills like music, art, programming, and design myself. So what if I don't make money? I can support myself anyway, and pursue my dreams. Even if no one recognizes it, even if it turns out to be just a joke, I still want to do it! ......After all, that's just how making games is, haha.
