Right now, it's, emmm, basically just purely observing the enthusiasm of young people? (Feeling a bit sentimental?) But I still don't really dare to participate in these kinds of things. Guess I'm still not that type of person, huh? (A bit dejected?) But since I came again, it seems okay? At least I got a photo (a bit happy) and also went along with the so-called 'photo collecting'. At least for now, it's a pretty fresh experience 😋

Then I watched about half an hour of freestyle dancing (though most of the time I was just listening to music, and of course, there were pretty cosplaying girls who would occasionally dance). But how should I put it, I got a bit tired of it? Let's see if there'll be other activities later.

There was a boy singing who seemed pretty good, and currently there's also a Tiga (cosplayer) who's not bad. Then I also sat down, and finally left around 5 PM. Went to the sports field to get some awesome shots, filmed the classic sprint transformation scene of Kurenai Gai (Ultraman Orb), so cool! All in all, it should be alright, right? Although I wasted some time, I still got to experience some human connection, and it also confirmed that I'm still that socially anxious person, not changed one bit, haha.
