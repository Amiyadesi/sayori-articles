## Friend Link Application

If you'd like to exchange friend links, you can publicly leave these details in the Twikoo comment section below. Comments will display normally. Once I see them, I'll manually add them to the list and reply directly in the comments after adding.

If your site or avatar is inaccessible for an extended period, the friend link will be hidden first; you can leave a message again once it's restored, and I'll re-display it.

```txt
名称：Amiya的书桌
地址：https://blog.sayori.org/
简介：Amiya_desi's blog, where a real person who wants to share things will be recorded. Welcome!
图标：https://blog.sayori.org/assets/profile/avatar-sayori.jpg
```

You can also link to the navigation site:

## Blog Networks, Organizations, and Directories

This page currently lists 11 public entry points. They are not all the same kind of thing: some are blog networks, long-term blogging pledges, or directories; others are site-identity or hosting-service entrances. Read each site's rules before joining or using it.

- [Travellings](https://www.travellings.cn/go.html): A network for hopping between independent websites.
- [Moe ICP](https://icp.gov.moe/): A site-identity and filing service for personal websites, not a commercial filing authority.
- [Forever Blog](https://www.foreverblog.cn/): A pledge and aggregation entry point for keeping personal blogs alive over the long term.
- [JiuChan](https://hi.jiuchan.org/docs): An independent-blog listing and linking entry point; follow its documentation for current rules.
- [Chinese Independent Blogs](https://github.com/timqian/chinese-independent-blogs): An open directory of Chinese independent blogs.
- [BlogFinder](https://bf.zzxworld.com/): A directory for discovering and browsing personal blogs.
- [Blog Friends Circle](https://www.boyouquan.com/): A Chinese blog discovery, subscription, and community entry point.
- [Firewood](https://firewood.news/): An aggregation entry point for independent blogs and Chinese web content.
- [BlogsClub Shuttle](https://www.blogsclub.org/shuttle.html): A blog-organization entry point for moving between participating independent sites.
- [Blogscn sponsorship page](https://blogscn.fun/pages/zhaoshang): A server-sponsorship and virtual-hosting provider page, not a blog directory.
- [Blog Alliance](https://bo.ke/): A blog-alliance entry point for discovering and aggregating independent blogs.

These links only record networks I have joined or collected. They do not mean that I guarantee any site's continued operation, content quality, or service terms.
