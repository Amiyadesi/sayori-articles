# About me and this blog

Hi, I’m **Amiya_desi**, an independent developer and a student still learning; I mainly use Godot for games and also enjoy building websites, tools, servers, and creative workflows

I started making games largely because of Undertale; I like works that make characters, mechanics, and a feeling stay with you, and I want to make something like that myself; time mechanics, multi-window interaction, and meta games are directions I keep returning to

This is my personal blog, **Amiya’s Desk**; think of it as an open desk with finished things, half-debugged problems, organized references, and thoughts that only became clear after some time

## What I write here

- **Game development**: Godot, gameplay experiments, Game Jam retrospectives, and the path from an idea to a playable build
- **Websites and self-hosting**: personal blogs, Docker, VPS, Cloudflare, and maintaining personal services
- **AI and workflows**: practical attempts with AI-assisted development, n8n, note organization, and content distribution
- **Tools and resources**: websites, tools, and open-source projects I use, build, or curate
- **Creative and personal notes**: video making, community observations, interests, and periodic reflection

## Why I write it down

When making things, the problems are usually concrete: why a mechanic feels flat, why a configuration fails, or whether a tool actually saves time

I want to keep the background, attempts, results, and unresolved parts clear; future me can see how I got here, and someone with a similar problem may find a useful reference

I use AI and automation tools, while still caring about understanding the mechanism, implementing things myself, and judging the result; learning and making are connected for me because what I learn should have a chance to become a work, and each work creates the next round of learning
