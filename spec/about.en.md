# About

This is Amiya_desi's personal blog.

About me: I'm a 19-year-old university student dreaming of becoming an indie game dev Master. My favorite genre is meta indie games, and I want to make one with "another layer of meaning beyond the screen."

I also like tinkering with things, including this blog. I'll share game development, learning, tools, and everyday thoughts here. I hope these records can help the data model who I am and work out things I can't figure out alone, so I want to write them all down.

I think a blog is here to carry its articles. After wrestling with the idea, I switched to a simpler style to make reading more comfortable. The story behind that change is [[blog-style-change|here]].

My publicly available works and projects are mainly on GitHub:

-   [WakeUpAtTheBorder](https://github.com/Amiyadesi/Wake-up-at-the-border): Godot 2D side-scrolling narrative game, second prize in the 3rd GodotHub Game Dev Competition
-   [TimeRuner](https://github.com/Amiyadesi/time-runner): C# action platformer, 5-second time rewind, Best Time-Themed Game Award at 034GameJam
-   [EnhanceSaveSystem](https://github.com/Amiyadesi/enahnce_save_system): Godot save plugin, supports AES-GCM, gzip, atomic writes, and version migration
-   [Mio's Windowanderer](https://github.com/Amiyadesi/ThatGameJam) / [少女们的假面舞会](https://github.com/Amiyadesi/ggj): Multi-window puzzle and text puzzle attempts made during GameJams

6.  `Personal Records`

    Some life, mood, interest, and periodic retrospectives. I'll keep a genuine human tone, but won't just dump raw diary entries as source material.
